# Artwork brief v3 — Camm Studios website (monochrome)

Use your image generation capability (imagegen skill, built-in image_gen tool)
to generate SEVEN images. After each generation, copy the file into this
workspace at the exact path given, overwriting the existing file.

Shared art direction for ALL images: monochrome retro-futurism. The scenes are
"space renaissance cowboy solarpunk", but rendered almost entirely in
GRAYSCALE: deep charcoal blacks, silvers, soft grays, near-white highlights,
like a masterful graphite-and-ink painting or a black-and-white sci-fi
paperback cover. Painterly texture, soft film grain, vast quiet compositions.

THE ONE COLOR: a single restrained cool signal-blue glow (#4e63ee, soft and
desaturated) may appear in ONE small element per image: a glowing window, a
star, a lamp, an instrument light. Everything else stays monochrome. The blue
element should feel like a quiet signal in a gray world.

Hard constraints for ALL images: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no
watermarks, no user interfaces, no screens, no readable faces, no warm orange
or sepia tones. Landscape orientation, widest available size.

1. `src/shared/assets/art/company-hero.png`
   A vast twilight desert prairie seen from a low ridge, all silver and
   charcoal. A single tiny rider on horseback far in the distance, heading
   toward a cluster of glass greenhouse domes whose panes glow soft
   signal-blue (the one color). A colossal ringed planet in grayscale rising
   over the horizon, first stars appearing. Keep the upper-left third of the
   sky quiet and empty for a headline.

2. `src/shared/assets/art/company-craft.png`
   Inside a glass dome at night, monochrome. A small friendly robot gardener
   prunes a tiny tree growing from a metal pot; the tree's leaves hold a faint
   signal-blue luminescence (the one color). Stars through the glass, silver
   lamp light, deep charcoal shadows. Intimate and quiet.

3. `src/shared/assets/art/company-404.png`
   A small round robot wearing a cowboy hat stands alone in an empty moonlit
   desert beside a leaning wooden signpost with blank boards, scratching its
   head. Grayscale night, huge sky, one signal-blue shooting star (the one
   color). Gently funny, lots of empty sky in the center.

4. `src/shared/assets/art/bronzed-hero.png`
   An enormous low sun over a calm dark sea, rendered in silvery grayscale
   like a solar eclipse photograph, surrounded by faint concentric etched
   measurement rings and tick arcs. Distant glass domes on a headland, one
   pane glowing signal-blue (the one color). Dramatic but monochrome. Space at
   the upper left for copy.

5. `src/shared/assets/art/stellar-hero.png`
   A deep charcoal night sky where a loose constellation of bright silver
   stars is joined by faint etched lines, above a dark mesa with a small brass
   telescope silhouette. One tent at the mesa's edge glows soft signal-blue
   (the one color). Vast, hopeful, quiet. Space at the upper left for copy.

6. `src/shared/assets/art/blog-uv.png`
   A close painterly still life of scientific solar instruments in grayscale:
   a heliograph, a sundial disc, and a small glass sphere on a stone ledge in
   hard light with sharp shadows. Inside the glass sphere, a faint signal-blue
   spark (the one color). Engraving mood, sci-fi edge.

7. `src/shared/assets/art/blog-369.png`
   An open blank paper journal and a pen on a small wooden desk floating
   gently in a grayscale starfield, a lantern casting silver light, a tiny
   ringed planet in the distance. The lantern flame burns faint signal-blue
   (the one color). Blank pages only, no writing.
