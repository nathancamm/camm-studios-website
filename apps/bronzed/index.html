Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bronzed free to use?

Bronzed offers a 3-day free trial so you can explore all features. After the trial, a subscription is required to continue using the app.

Will Bronzed stop me from getting sunburnt?

No app can guarantee that. Bronzed gives you guidance based on the live UV index, weather, and a general dermatology safe-time model for your skin type — it is an estimate, not a guarantee. Your real response to the sun varies, so always follow official sun-safety advice, wear sunscreen, and stop if your skin feels like it's burning.

How does Bronzed know my safe time?

During onboarding you tell Bronzed how your skin reacts to strong sun (your Fitzpatrick skin type). Bronzed combines that with the real-time UV index for your location to estimate a safe tanning window and pace your session.

Is my data private?

Your profile and session history are stored locally on your device. We use anonymous analytics to improve the app and never collect your name or email. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Find Bronzed and tap Cancel. You can also manage subscriptions here.
